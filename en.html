FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a business valuation cost?

It depends on the size and structure of the company. After a 20-minute call I send a fixed scope and fee — no hourly billing, no hidden charges.

How long does preparing a business for sale take?

A valuation usually takes 1–2 weeks. A full sale package (valuation report, financial model, investment memorandum, deal documents) — from 3–4 weeks, depending on how ready your accounts are.

Is it confidential?

Yes. Every engagement starts with an NDA. Company data is never shared with third parties without your written consent — including the very fact that the business is up for sale.

Do you work with businesses outside the UAE?

Yes — the CIS and the Middle East. Cross-border documents are prepared bilingually (Russian + English).

Do you guarantee the sale?

No — and do not trust anyone who does. What I guarantee is a defensible price, a quality document package and an honest picture of the risks. That is what ultimately decides whether a deal closes.
